Acme Corp Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.

Gifts and hospitality: employees may not accept gifts worth more than 50 USD from vendors or customers. Any gift above this limit must be declined or reported to the compliance team within five working days.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to your manager before you take part in any related decision.
