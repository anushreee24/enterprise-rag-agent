HR Confidential - 2026 Salary Bands

Software Engineer I: 8 to 12 lakh INR per year. Software Engineer II: 12 to 20 lakh INR per year. Senior Engineer: 20 to 35 lakh INR per year.

The annual bonus pool is 8 percent of base salary for all grades, paid in April. Promotion cycles run in March and September. Compensation data in this document must not be shared outside the HR leadership team.
