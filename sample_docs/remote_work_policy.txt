Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during which employees should be reachable on the company chat tool.

Equipment: the company provides one laptop and one monitor per remote employee. A home-office stipend of 300 USD is available once per calendar year, claimed through the expenses portal with receipts.

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.
