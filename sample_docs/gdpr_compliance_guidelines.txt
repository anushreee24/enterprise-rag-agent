Compliance Guidelines - Personal Data Handling

Personal data may only be collected for a specified purpose and must not be kept longer than necessary. Customer personal data is retained for a maximum of 24 months after the contract ends, then securely deleted.

Data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) must be answered within 30 days. Any transfer of personal data outside the European Economic Area requires a documented legal basis, such as standard contractual clauses, approved by the legal team.
