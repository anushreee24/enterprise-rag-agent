Finance Confidential - Q3 FY2026 Forecast

Projected Q3 revenue is 42 million USD, up 11 percent over Q2. Cloud services is the fastest growing line at 19 percent growth. Operating margin is forecast at 17 percent.

Risks: two large customers are renegotiating renewal terms, which could reduce Q4 revenue by up to 3 million USD. Hiring is paused for non-engineering roles until the Q3 close.
